Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026
Çakmanus · mustilispor2002@hotmail.com
Android package: com.cakmanus.duckner

Microphone and audio

Duckner requests microphone permission to detect the note you play, its frequency and tuning deviation. Incoming sound is processed only on your device, briefly in memory. It is not saved as an audio recording, uploaded or shared. The microphone may remain active while the reference tone plays. Listening and reference playback stop when the app goes into the background.

Local settings

Your language, reference frequency, costume choice and internal setup/permission flags are stored locally. They remain until you change them, clear app storage or uninstall Duckner. Android cloud backup is disabled in the app manifest. Duckner does not create accounts and includes no advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs or in-app purchases. It does not request internet permission or transmit app data to a developer server.

Your choices and deletion

You may deny or revoke microphone permission through Android settings; pitch measurement then becomes unavailable. You can change language, reference frequency and available costume in the app. Clear app storage or uninstall to delete local preferences. There are no developer-held app recordings or user accounts to delete.

Support and platform services

If you email support, the address and information you voluntarily send are used to respond to your request. Correspondence is handled through the email service and retained only as needed to handle the request or meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by contacting the address above. Do not send unnecessary sensitive information. Google Play, Android and your email provider may independently process data under their own policies; this is separate from Duckner's on-device pitch analysis.

Policy updates

Changes to app data handling will be reflected in this policy, its update date and the Google Play Data safety declaration. For privacy questions, contact Çakmanus at mustilispor2002@hotmail.com.
